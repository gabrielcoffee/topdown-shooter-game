# Zombie Chamber — itch page (research-based)

## O que a pesquisa diz

Fontes: [itch.io blog oficial — "Make your game pages beautiful"](https://itch.io/blog/34049/make-your-game-pages-beautiful-to-boost-your-sales), [arimia — itch page guide](https://arimiadev.com/how-to-make-your-itch-io-page-stand-out/), [página do Vampire Survivors](https://poncle.itch.io/vampire-survivors) (maior hit do gênero horda-survival, nasceu no itch), [topo da tag top-down shooter + zombies](https://itch.io/games/tag-top-down-shooter/tag-zombies).

1. **GIFs vendem, texto apoia.** "People don't like to read" (itch blog). 3-5 GIFs de ação = o argumento principal; a descrição só confirma. GIFs de 5s são "massively sharable".
2. **Estrutura vencedora** (arimia): tagline de 1 linha → resumo curto → bullets de conteúdo → detalhes/controles. Página pode ser longa, mas escaneável.
3. **Vampire Survivors**: hook de 2 frases → 1 parágrafo factual do loop → **bullets com NÚMEROS** (7 characters, 22 weapons, 12 power-ups...) → dicas iniciais → FAQ. Zero prosa poética. Números concretos = conteúdo percebido.
4. **Sem comparação vaga** ("é tipo Zelda com Dark Souls" — itch blog manda evitar). UMA âncora precisa de nostalgia ok, desde que seguida de especificidade.
5. Taglines do topo da tag: curtas, diretas, uma ideia ("Slay zombies back to back!", "tactical top-down multiplayer shooter").

## Página pronta (colar no itch)

**Tagline (campo "short description"):**

> The CoD Zombies loop, top-down: points, wall-buys, mystery box, endless waves.

**Descrição:**

> Kill for cash. Spend it on wall guns, locked doors, or the mystery box — then hold on, because every 5th wave is a NIGHTMARE wave and the horde starts running.
>
> Zombie Chamber is a top-down zombie wave shooter built on the classic round-based survival loop: every hit pays, every door opens deeper into the map, and the waves never stop scaling. Aim matters — spread, recoil and counter-strafing work like a real shooter. Fire spreads. Shotguns pierce. The lights bend around the walls.
>
> **In the chamber:**
>
> - 4 guns + knife, grenades and molotovs
> - Mystery box with live odds — it leans toward guns the fuller your pockets get
> - Wall-buys, paid doors, pushable crates
> - 7 power-up drops: Nuke, Insta-Kill, Max Ammo, Fire Sale, Double Points, Freeze, Carpenter
> - 3 zombie types; endless waves, NIGHTMARE every 5th
> - Save & quit mid-run, best-wave record tracked
> - Dynamic lighting, positional audio with real reverb, CRT filter
> - English, Português, Español
>
> **Controls:**
>
> - WASD move · Shift sprint · mouse aim/shoot
> - R reload · Q quick-knife · 1-5 / wheel swap items
> - E buy & interact · G drop gun · Esc pause
>
> **Tips for your first run:**
>
> - The knife pays the most per kill — early waves are payday.
> - Sprinting means you can't shoot. The horde knows.
> - A molotov thrown at a doorway is a wall that burns.

**Tags:** action, zombies, top-down-shooter, arcade, singleplayer, pixel-art, 2d, survival
