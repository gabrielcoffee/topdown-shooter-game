## Estilo 1 — Direto / arcade

**Tagline:** CoD Zombies, top-down, made by one guy.

> Zombie Chamber is a top-down zombie wave shooter with the full CoD Zombies loop: earn cash per hit, buy guns off the wall, gamble it all at the mystery box, open doors deeper into the map, and survive nightmare waves that flip the horde into runners. Molotovs burn, shotguns pierce, power-ups drop — nuke, insta-kill, fire sale, max ammo. How far can you get?
>
> - 4 guns + knife, grenades and molotovs
> - Mystery box with shifting odds, wall-buys, 7 power-up drops
> - Waves scale forever; every 5th is a NIGHTMARE wave
> - Save & continue mid-run, records tracked
> - CRT looks, dynamic lighting, full positional audio
> - Made solo in Lua/LÖVE

## Estilo 2 — Nostalgia / história do dev

**Tagline:** I spent months rebuilding the CoD Zombies feeling — top-down, $1.

> Remember World at War zombies? Saving every point for the mystery box, the panic-buy off the wall, the wave counter climbing past what you thought you could handle? I couldn't stop thinking about it — so I rebuilt that whole feeling as a top-down shooter, alone, in Lua.
>
> Everything that made the loop click is here: cash per hit, wall-buys, the box (it rolls better guns the more desperate you are), locked doors hiding the rest of the map, power-ups tumbling out of runners, and nightmare waves every 5th round that turn the shuffle into a sprint. No filler, no menus between you and the horde. One map, one life, one record to beat.
>
> If a round ever ended and you whispered "one more" — this is for you.

## Estilo 3 — Atmosférico / horror

**Tagline:** The chamber is sealed. The waves are not.

> Somewhere underground there's a room that hums with old electricity. The doors are locked from the inside. The dead find their way in anyway — slow at first, shambling through the torchlight. Then faster. Then all at once, under a purple sky that means the fifth wave has come again.
>
> Zombie Chamber is a top-down survival shooter about holding a place that doesn't want to be held. Scrape money off every corpse. Spend it on wall guns, on sealed doors, on a box that takes your cash and laughs before it gives you anything back. Fire spreads where you throw it. Light bends around the walls. The heartbeat you hear at low health is yours.
>
> There is no ending. There's only the number you left behind.
